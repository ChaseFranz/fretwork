**Effort:** M. The merge itself (five conflicts: `analyze.py`, `functions/instruments.py`, `functions/timestamp.py`, `parsers/chart_parser.py`, `parsers/mid_parser.py`), then the site: a sixth sheet that is not a chart sheet, the curve recipe for two families, the words for an additive formula, the drift set emptied, and the fixture and harness taught upstream's new behaviour.

## Goal

The site says what the engine says after upstream's refit: every D rescored by formula v3, every tier recalibrated, the vocals numbers rebuilt on the simplified formula, and upstream's new per-song **Band** placement on each song page, without the Band sheet pretending to be a chart.

## Why

Upstream rebuilt three things between 2026-09-19 and 09-30. The fret formula went additive (`D = (N + V) * CoV * STAM` where v2 multiplied N by V, "Data analysis showed Drums-style structure fit better across the board"), which rescales every guitar, bass and keys number: the hardest Expert guitar chart in the Local library reads 79.68 where v2 read 1,233.95, and the median Expert guitar chart 13.34 where it read 34.08. Vocals were simplified (`D = (P * R + S) * CoV * STAM`: articulation `A` and the `ShortFrac` metric are gone, `R` is `sqrt(Pitches) * maxPitch / MID_PITCH`, the syllable weight and the CoV scale are 1), which moves every vocals number the other way, the median Expert from 8.33 to 21.65. Every remap table and both CalcTier constants of all three families were refit with their code, drums included, so drum tiers moved although drum D did not (`BASE_D` 9 to 10.243, `LN_INC` 0.196 to 0.1602). A site still on v2 would rank charts by numbers the engine no longer produces, and would print tier boundaries that no longer exist.

Upstream also added a cross-instrument placement per song (`functions/band_formula.py`, a `Band` tab in the spreadsheet: the mean of the song's two hardest core placements, floored, over `RemapDiff` and over `CalcTier`, where core is the lead (guitar, else keys) plus bass and drums, needing two of them), preliminary `.sng` and `_rb3con` readers, and a general refactor that moved where outputs land.

## Current state

Measured on the merged tree with the Local library rebuilt 2026-10-01 21:55 (2,272 songs, 34 packs, no build errors): 18,838 chart rows on five sheets (Guitar 8,781, Bass 5,102, Keys 256, Drums 3,820, Vocals 879; 18,726 distinct), against 18,807 before, the 31 new drum rows from upstream's drums note-count fix. Expert CalcTier now runs 0 to 14 on guitar (median 4), 0 to 12 on bass, 0 to 7 on keys, 0 to 8 on drums (median 3, where it was 4) and 0 to 8 on vocals (median 3, where it was 2). The sixth sheet, `Band`, holds 1,313 rows, one per song with two core parts at Expert: 805 of them G/B/D/V, 356 G/B, 63 G/B/D/K/V, 55 G/B/D, 31 G/D, 2 G/B/V, with `CalcBandTier` from 0 to 12 (median 3).

Upstream's `Methodology.md` now agrees with its code: the seven drifts section 23 reported are all gone, so `methodology.KNOWN_DRIFT` is empty and the page carries no drift note. Two upstream changes break the fork where nothing fails loudly: a relative output folder from `config.OUTPUT_DIRS` is now anchored to the repo rather than to the working directory (`timestamp.project_path`), which made `tools/ingest_pack.py` and `tests/pipeline_test.py` write into the repo from a temporary directory, and a folder holding both `notes.mid` and `notes.chart` now reads the **mid**, where the comment in `build.py` said chart since the fork began.

## Design

1. **The Band sheet is a song per row, so it is not served as a chart.** `instruments.CHART_SHEETS` is every sheet but `Band`; `web/frames.split_band(frames)` pops it and `frames.band_rows(band)` reduces it to `{SongKey: {'remap', 'tier', 'instruments'}}`. `page.build()` splits before anything else reads the sheets, so the table, the percentile, the counts, the library page, the game pages and the lists see exactly the five chart sheets they saw before. The placement is joined onto the song pages by `SongKey` (`song_facts(sheets, bands)` stores `fact['band']`) and read out as one more sentence (`UI['song_band']`, after the parts), which is the only place on the site it appears. Rejected: a sixth tab in the table (its rows have no `Code`, no `Level`, no `D` and no graph, so every column the table leads with would be empty and a click would open nothing); a `Band` column on the chart sheets (the placement is per song, and a song's rows are spread over five sheets); ignoring it (the engine's own cross-instrument number, which is what a band player wants, and it costs one sentence).

2. **`SongKey` on the band row.** Upstream's `_band_row` carried no identity, so the fork ports one: `analyze.py` passes `song_key=song.get('song_key')` and `BAND_COLUMN_ORDER` ends with `SongKey`. No `NotesHash`, since a band row is a song and not a chart. That is the minimum the join needs and it is the key that survives a re-download, so a song page keeps its placement when a pack is replaced.

3. **The curve recipe follows the formula.** `web/graph.py` now writes `{'sum': {'nps': 1, 'vps': 1}}` for the fret family (v2 wrote `{'geo': ['nps', 'vps']}`) and `{'sum': {'pps': R, 'sps': 1}}` for vocals (v2 carried `R * A` and `S_WEIGHT`). The file version stays 2: the shape did not change, only the recipe inside it, and `graph.js` already reads both forms, so the page needed no edit at all. `tests/test_graph_json.py` and `tests/page/graph.js` share one pinned vector, re-derived for v3, and `tests/test_bundle.py` reconstructs every family's ~D from its JSON against `curves.py`.

4. **The anchor for outputs is overridable.** `timestamp.base_dir()` reads `FRETWORK_BASE_DIR` and falls back to upstream's `BASE_DIR`, and `project_path` goes through it. `tools/ingest_pack.py` sets it from its working directory (its documented contract: `caches/` and `metrics/` land where it was run, and `build.py` and `analyze.py` are its children), and `tests/pipeline_test.py` sets it per stage, so the harness can never write into the repo again. Upstream's default is untouched. Rejected: a `--out-dir` flag on build and analyze (upstream's to add, and the harness would still have to pass it four times); the harness passing `--cache`/`--xlsx` paths only (the backup CSV, the errors CSV and the pack registry have no flag).

5. **The fixture learns the new behaviour, from the table.** `fmt='both'` now means the mid wins: the fixture writes the divergent stream into the chart and the canonical one into the mid, `Song.source_format` reads `'mid'`, and the harness asserts the cache took the mid. The errors CSV holds a row per folder with both formats (upstream's `MultipleChart` warning) as well as the broken mid, so `Library.error_rows` is the count the summary line and the CSV are checked against, and the dropped "No usable chart/mid" summary line is dropped here too. The backup CSV's cells are `missing` where a `diff_*` tag is absent (upstream stopped defaulting them to `'-1'`) and `diff_band` is written for every song, so `Library.backup_rows` says so. `Library.band_songs` is the Band sheet's expected rows, derived from the same `SONGS` table as every other count, and the old backup header the migration test writes is a prefix of `BACKUP_COLUMNS` rather than a filter of it, since `diff_band` arrived after `diff_drums` and `diff_vocals`.

6. **The drift set is empty, and the page says nothing.** `methodology.KNOWN_DRIFT = ()`. The table lookup now skips the prose between a heading and its table (upstream added a refit note under the vocals heading), the three mutation cases in `tests/test_methodology.py` are live numbers from the refit tables, and `tests/page/narrow.js` and the harness read 27 display formulas where they read 29. The page's drift block is asserted both ways: absent while nothing drifts, rendered when a drift exists (a patched `methodology.load`), so an upstream refit without its table is still noticed on the page and not only in the terminal.

7. **The words for an additive formula.** `labels.COLUMN_HELP['D']` and the explainer say the fret family adds N to V and that vocals multiply pitch movement by the register and add the syllables; `A` and `ShortFrac` are gone from the labels, the curve alt text reads "their sum", and `RemapBandDiff`, `CalcBandTier` and `Instruments` have labels and help although no served sheet carries them yet. The CalcTier help still prints all three constant pairs from the modules, so the refit numbers are on the page without an edit.

8. **The containers are upstream's, and the site needs nothing.** `.sng` and `_rb3con` songs enter the cache keyed by the container file; the registry join takes the first path component under the library root, which is still the pack folder, and `ini_updater.is_container_song` keeps the write-back away from them. The Local library is chart-only folders (`tools/sanitize_songs.py`), so none are in it today; nothing on the site branches on the format beyond the `source_format` the graph header prints.

## What this leaves out

- **A Band tab on the site.** Design 1 says why the Band sheet is not a chart sheet; a band view of its own (a sixth sheet keyed on `SongKey`, with the song page as its row click) is a section of its own if the maintainer wants one, and it needs the table to handle a sheet with no `Code` first.
- **The 2x drum picture and the band graph.** Neither exists upstream as a curve; the drums file is still the 1x picture.
- **A `.sng` or `_rb3con` library.** Upstream calls both preliminary and the fork hosts chart-only folders; when one lands in a pack, `tools/ingest_pack.py` and the sanitizer are what need the work, not the site.
- **Anything scored in the fork.** Unchanged from 23: `D`, the bins and the tiers are upstream's, and the drift check exists to prove the site's tables are the engine's.
